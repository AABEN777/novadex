How it works
01You deposit USDC into a Circle-managed wallet. It leaves your wallet but does not reach theirs.
02They mark the work delivered. You confirm. The funds release the moment both agree.
03If the deadline passes, funds settle to whoever actually held up their end. Nobody stays stuck.
04Disagree? Raise a dispute and both sides submit evidence for review.
